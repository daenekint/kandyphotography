K&Y PHOTOGRAPHY WEBSITE
========================

1. Upload the complete contents of this folder to the public web root of kandy-photography.com.
2. Put your real photographs in /images/ using the filenames already referenced by the HTML.
3. Replace the # placeholders for Facebook and Instagram with the real profile URLs.
4. The contact form currently uses mailto as a simple fallback. For reliable website forms, connect a form service or your hosting's mail handler.
5. Submit https://kandy-photography.com/sitemap.xml to Google Search Console.
6. Keep the business name, contact details and locations accurate. Do not create location pages for places you do not serve.
7. Before launch, add a privacy/cookie/legal page appropriate to the actual tracking and contact tools you use.

The pages are intentionally lightweight and responsive. The image files are placeholders and are not included.
